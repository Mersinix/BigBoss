**Coffee Owner**

↕

**Maintenance Account**

↕

**Admin**

Coffee Owner creates review

↓

Maintenance account sees review

↓

Admin Maintenance Reviews sees review

If Maintenance reports review

↓

Admin sees it as reported

If Admin deletes review

↓

The review disappears/updates appropriately for Maintenance and Coffee Owner.

No manual refresh should be required where the existing realtime infrastructure supports it.
