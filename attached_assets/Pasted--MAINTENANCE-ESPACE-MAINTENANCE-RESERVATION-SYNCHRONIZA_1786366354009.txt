# 2. Espace Maintenance → PLANNING

The Planning page currently has:

**Aujourd'hui**

**À venir**

**Passées**

Reservations are currently appearing incorrectly under **À venir**.

They must be dispatched according to their actual reservation date/time:

### Aujourd'hui

Reservations whose scheduled date is today.

### À venir

Reservations whose scheduled date is after today.

### Passées

Reservations whose scheduled date is before today.

This must use the real reservation date/time.

Do not classify reservations based only on their status.

For example:

* A confirmed reservation scheduled today → **Aujourd'hui**
* A confirmed reservation scheduled tomorrow → **À venir**
* A completed/confirmed reservation from yesterday → **Passées**

The date-based classification must remain correct regardless of the reservation status.

Preserve the existing Planning design.

---

# 9. RESCHEDULING WORKFLOW

The rescheduling workflow must become:

### Step 1 — Maintenance Agent

Maintenance agent chooses:

**Reprogrammée**

↓

Calendar/date-time modal opens.

↓

Agent selects a new date/time.

↓

Agent submits the proposed change.

---

### Step 2 — Coffee Owner

The Coffee Owner receives the proposed modification.

The original reservation must not simply be overwritten without confirmation.

The Coffee Owner must be able to:

**Confirmer la modification**

or

**Refuser la modification**

---

### Step 3 — Confirmation

If Coffee Owner confirms:

The reservation's scheduled date/time is updated to the proposed date/time.

Both sides must see the updated reservation.

---

### Step 4 — Rejection

If Coffee Owner rejects:

The proposed date/time must not replace the existing confirmed reservation date/time.

Both sides must see that the rescheduling request was rejected according to the existing reservation/status design.
