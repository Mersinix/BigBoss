5. Pending Approval Flow

The current Pending Approval experience must be simplified.

Current Behavior

When a newly created account is waiting for approval, the application currently shows several approval screens and banners such as:

Awaiting approval

Pending account

Prices hidden

Login to view prices

Pending approval screen

Return Home

There are also components like:

pending-approval-screen.tsx

PendingApprovalScreen

App.tsx approval logic

and possibly other related code.

New Required Behavior

When a user creates a new account that is not yet approved:

After registration:

Redirect the user directly to the Landing Page.

Do not redirect them into their Marketplace.

Do not open the Coffee Owner panel.

Do not show the pending dashboard.

Do not show:

Awaiting Approval banner
Pending Approval screen
Hidden prices message
Login to view prices message
Any other pending-account UI inside Marketplace

Instead:

Immediately display a simple professional confirmation modal on the Landing Page.

Example meaning (rewrite professionally):

Your registration has been received successfully.
Our team will review your account shortly. Once your account has been approved, we will contact you by phone or email. After approval, you will be able to sign in and access your dashboard.

The wording may be improved, but the meaning should remain the same.

After closing the modal:

The user simply remains on the Landing Page.

Until approved by Admin:

If they try to log in:

They should not access Marketplace.

Instead:

Show the same professional approval modal.

No Marketplace should open before approval.
