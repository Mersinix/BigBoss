# Category Discount Promotion Scope

Example:

**Category Discount**

20% OFF

* Category A
* Category B

This is incorrect.

---

# Expected Behavior

A Category Discount must only affect the products belonging to the supplier who created that promotion.

Example:

Supplier A creates:

20% OFF

Categories:

* Coffee
* Drinks

Only Supplier A's products inside those categories should receive the promotion.

Supplier B

Supplier C

Supplier D

must not receive that promotion, even if they sell products in the exact same categories.

---

# Promotion Scope

* Promotion Owner (Supplier)
* Selected Category
* Product belonging to that Supplier

All three conditions must be satisfied before applying the promotion.
