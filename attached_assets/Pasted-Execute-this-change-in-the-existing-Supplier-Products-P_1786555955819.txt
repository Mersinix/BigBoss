### Required fix — Live Preview must match “Variants in pack” exactly

In the **Edit Pack** modal, the **Live preview → Includes** section is currently showing variants that are not actually selected in **“Variants in pack”**.

Example:

In **Variants in pack**, the supplier currently has only:

* `Lait demi ecrémé – Delice Holding`
* `6-Pack`
* `Cinnamon`
* quantity `2`

But the **Live preview → Includes** currently shows:

* `1× FANTA 25cl (BLANC) — Gajou`
* `2× Lait demi ecrémé – Delice Holding (Cinnamon) — 6-Pack`

This is incorrect.

### Correct behavior

The **Includes** section of the Live preview must be a direct representation of the **currently selected variants in “Variants in pack”**.

Therefore, in the example above, the preview must show ONLY:

`2× Lait demi ecrémé – Delice Holding (Cinnamon) — 6-Pack`

The unselected `FANTA 25cl (BLANC) — Gajou` must completely disappear.

### Synchronization rules

* Every selected variant in **Variants in pack** → appears in **Includes**.
* Every unselected variant → does NOT appear in **Includes**.
* If a variant is removed from **Variants in pack**, it must immediately disappear from the preview.
* If a variant is added back, it must immediately appear in the preview.
* The quantity shown in the preview must match the selected quantity.
* Do not display previously selected/stale variants.
* Do not display variants merely because they were historically part of the Pack.
* The preview must use the exact same current variant selection that drives the Pack price and stock calculations.
