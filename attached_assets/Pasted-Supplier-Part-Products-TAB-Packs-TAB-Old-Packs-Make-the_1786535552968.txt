Supplier Part > Products TAB > Packs TAB > Old Packs

Make the following modification to the existing **Old Packs** behavior:

When the Supplier opens **Edit Pack** from the **Old Packs** page, the Pack modal currently still shows products that are no longer active/available in the Pack's **Live preview → Includes** section.

This should be corrected.

Requirements:

1. If a product/variant used by a Pack has been deleted, removed, or is no longer active, it must **no longer appear anywhere as an active Pack component**.
2. In the **Old Packs → Edit Pack** modal, inactive/removed products must not remain in the Pack's product/component relation.
3. In **Live preview → Includes**, show **only currently active products/variants** that are still valid members of the Pack.
4. The Supplier must be able to remove obsolete/inactive Pack components from the Pack and then add new active products to the Pack.
5. Do not recreate or restore deleted/inactive products just because they were previously part of the Pack.
6. Keep all currently working Pack behavior unchanged:

   * Pack pricing
   * Pack stock calculation
   * Pack images
   * Flash image
   * Variants / flavors / sizes
   * Old Packs lifecycle
   * New Packs behavior
   * Product deletion/removal behavior
   * Realtime synchronization
7. Make sure the cleanup is synchronized everywhere the Pack is displayed, so obsolete products do not continue appearing in Pack details, previews, marketplace/store views, or other Pack-related interfaces.
8. Preserve the existing UI/design. Do not redesign the modal or remove any existing functionality that is unrelated to this requirement.
9. Before making changes, inspect the existing Pack/product relationship and deletion/removal logic and verify how inactive products are currently represented.
10. After the changes, verify the complete flow:

    * Product is active → appears in Pack.
    * Product is removed/deleted/deactivated → no longer appears as an active Pack component.
    * Old Pack opens correctly.
    * Obsolete component can be removed.
    * New active product can be added.
    * Pack preview shows only valid active components.
    * Existing Pack price/stock behavior remains correct.
    * Changes synchronize without requiring a page refresh.

Make only the changes necessary for this requirement and do not break or modify unrelated parts of the application.
