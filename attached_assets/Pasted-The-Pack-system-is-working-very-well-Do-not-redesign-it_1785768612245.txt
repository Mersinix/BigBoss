1. Pack stock synchronization after order confirmation

Currently, when a Coffee Owner orders a Pack and the Supplier approves the order, the Pack stock is updated, but the stock of the individual variants/flavors is not always synchronized correctly.

Update the synchronization so that:

When a Pack order is confirmed/approved by the Supplier, the correct stock is deducted from every included product.
The deduction must happen at the exact Variant + Flavor level.
Only the selected flavor(s) used for the Pack should be decremented.
No unrelated variants or flavors should be modified.

Example:

If a Pack contains:

2 × Product A → Variant Medium → Flavor Toffeh
1 × Product B → Variant Gajou → Flavor BLANC

After the Supplier confirms the order:

Product A → Medium → Toffeh stock decreases by 2
Product B → Gajou → BLANC stock decreases by 1

No other flavors should change.

Realtime synchronization
