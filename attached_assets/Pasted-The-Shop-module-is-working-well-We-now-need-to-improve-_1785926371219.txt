# Coffee Owner → Shop → Product Cards

We need to reorganize the mapped Product Cards.

The goal is to make the card represent the product itself, while supplier-specific information (such as pricing and promotions) is displayed inside the Product Details modal where the user chooses a supplier.

---

# 1. Remove Product Price From the Product Card

A single product can be sold by multiple suppliers.

Because of that, the Product Card should no longer display a price.

Remove the displayed price from the mapped Product Card.

The Coffee Owner should only see supplier prices after opening the Product Details modal.

Do not remove pricing from the Product Details modal.

---

# 2. Remove Promotion Pricing From the Product Card

Do not display promotion prices or discount information directly on the Product Card.

Instead:

If at least one supplier has an active promotion for that product:

Display a small **"On Sale"** badge on the Product Card.

The badge should use the same visual style already used in the Flash module (red promotion indicator).

Only indicate that promotions exist.

Do not display:

* discounted price
* original price
* percentage
* promotion details

on the Product Card.

---

# 3. Product Details Modal

Promotion information belongs inside the Product Details modal.

When the Coffee Owner opens a product:

Display each supplier's promotion only in the correct supplier section.

Promotion information must stay associated with:

* the correct supplier
* the correct product

Do not display promotion information outside its related supplier.

Keep the existing Product Details workflow.

---
