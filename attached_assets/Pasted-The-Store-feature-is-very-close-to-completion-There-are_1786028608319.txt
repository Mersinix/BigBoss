# 1. Fix Product Pricing Inside a Store

When browsing products inside a Store, the pricing must always come from **the supplier who owns that Store**.

### Product Card

Currently, a product with an active promotion displays an incorrect price.

Instead, the Product Card should display:

* The promotional (discounted) price as the main price.
* The original price with a strikethrough.
* The distance at the end of the same row.

Example:

**18.00 DT** ~~20.00 DT~~ ........ **11.3 km**

Use the existing global currency formatting (currency symbol on the right).

---

# 2. Fix Product Details Modal Inside a Store

The Product Details modal is still using marketplace data instead of Store-scoped data.

This must be corrected.

When a Coffee Owner opens a product **from inside a Store**, every piece of information in the modal must come **only from the supplier who owns that Store**.

That includes:

* Product price
* Promotional price
* Original price
* Variants
* Flavors
* Sizes
* Stock
* Promotions
* Distance
* Reviews
* Supplier information

The header price must be calculated using that supplier's product only.

Do **not** calculate it from another supplier or from marketplace data.

---

# 3. Remove Marketplace Supplier Comparison

The following marketplace behavior must not appear inside a Store Product Details modal.

Remove the multi-supplier comparison completely.

For example, instead of showing:

* Café Bondin
* Café Ellouze

the modal should display only the supplier that owns the Store currently being viewed.

There must be:

* one supplier
* one supplier section
* one supplier's variants
* one supplier's prices
* one supplier's promotions
* one supplier's stock

No marketplace comparison should remain inside a Store.

The marketplace supplier comparison must continue working exactly as it does today **only in the Shop Product Details modal**.

---

# 4. Keep Marketplace Behavior Unchanged

Do **not** modify the Product Details modal opened from the Shop marketplace.

Only the Store Product Details modal should become supplier-scoped.

The Shop must continue to:

* compare suppliers

* sort suppliers

exactly as it works today.
