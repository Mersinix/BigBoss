# 1. FIX PACK VARIANT DISTRIBUTION IN THE COFFEE OWNER CART

Current behavior:

The Coffee Owner purchases a pack such as:

**CHICHA PACK**

Café Bondin
د.ت70.00 per pack

Included products:

**CHICHA**

* CHIKH · شيشا · chicha
* Quantity selected according to pack quantity

**FANTA 25cl**

* Ananas · Gajou
* Citron · Gajou
* BLANC · Gajou

The Coffee Owner chooses the flavor distribution for the FANTA product.

For example:

### Pack quantity = 3

The Coffee Owner selects:

* 1 × Ananas
* 1 × Citron
* 1 × BLANC

This is correct.

But currently, after adding the pack to the cart, the cart incorrectly transforms the selected distribution into:

* 3 × Ananas
* 3 × Citron
* 3 × BLANC

This is WRONG.

The cart must preserve the **exact variant distribution selected by the Coffee Owner**.

So for 3 packs, the cart must display:

**FANTA 25cl**

Fanta · مشروبات · Gazouz

* 1× Ananas · Gajou
* 1× Citron · Gajou
* 1× BLANC · Gajou

with the pack quantity shown as:

**3**

---

### Second example

If the Coffee Owner buys **4 packs** and selects:

* 2 × Ananas
* 1 × Citron
* 1 × BLANC

Then the cart MUST display:

**FANTA 25cl**

* 2× Ananas · Gajou
* 1× Citron · Gajou
* 1× BLANC · Gajou

and the pack quantity:

**4**

It must NOT transform this into:

* 4 × Ananas
* 4 × Citron
* 4 × BLANC

---

### Important rule

The pack quantity and the variant-distribution quantities are two different concepts.

For a pack quantity of `N`, the sum of the selected variant quantities for each configurable included product must equal `N`.

The cart must preserve these exact quantities.

Do NOT automatically multiply every selected variant quantity by the pack quantity.

The cart should represent:

**Pack quantity = number of complete packs**

while the included-product variant quantities represent:

**how those packs were distributed among the available variants.**

## Cart increment/decrement behavior for packs

When the Coffee Owner increments the pack quantity in the cart:

Example:

Current:

**3 packs**

FANTA:

* 1 × Ananas
* 1 × Citron
* 1 × BLANC

When increasing to **4 packs**, the variant distribution must remain logically consistent and must NOT simply duplicate every variant into 4.

The expected result for this specific existing distribution should become:

* 2 × Ananas
* 1 × Citron
* 1 × BLANC

Total = 4.

In other words, when the pack quantity changes, preserve the existing distribution as much as possible and add/remove the minimum necessary quantity from the distribution instead of multiplying every variant quantity.

Similarly, when decrementing:

Current:

**4 packs**

* 2 × Ananas
* 1 × Citron
* 1 × BLANC

When decrementing to **3 packs**, the distribution should become:

* 1 × Ananas
* 1 × Citron
* 1 × BLANC

Total = 3.

Do not reset the distribution unnecessarily.

The system should always keep the selected distribution synchronized with the pack quantity.

## Important grouping rule

The grouping should happen only when the items represent the **same underlying product from the same supplier**.

Different suppliers must remain separate.

Different products must remain separate.

Do not accidentally merge unrelated products simply because they have the same name.

The grouping must respect the existing product/supplier identity and variant structure already used by the application.

# Variant controls

Inside the grouped product:

Each variant must have its own:

* quantity
* decrement button
* increment button
* delete button

Changing one variant's quantity must affect **only that variant**.

For example:

If:

* 1kg Bag = 1
* 500g Bag = 1
* Espresso Shot = 1

and the Coffee Owner increments 500g Bag:

* 1kg Bag = 1
* 500g Bag = 2
* Espresso Shot = 1

Do NOT change the other variants.

If the Coffee Owner deletes the 500g Bag variant, only that variant should disappear. The parent product must remain in the cart as long as at least one variant remains.

If the last remaining variant of a product is deleted, then the entire product entry should be removed from the cart.

The cart total must always update correctly after every variant quantity change or deletion.
